    HOW TO PLAY

    1. You have 60 seconds to break through four security layers, in order.
    2. Each layer is a different puzzle: decrypt a code, route a network, decode a pattern.
    3. Follow the rule on screen. Use the keyboard, or tap and drag on a touch screen.
    4. A wrong answer costs 5 seconds or more. The clock never stops.
    5. Clear all four layers for ACCESS GRANTED and unlock the next level. Higher levels are harder.
    6. Clearing a node earns hacking points. Spend them before a run on extra time, a shield or a hint.
